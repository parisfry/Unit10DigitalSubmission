PROJECTION PLANNING.

Selecting which of my digital outcomes should be in the final projection video for the installation.

Projection Option 01

Dithering videos - these were not very effective as the particle movement is not very noticable.

Projection Option 02

Ascii videos - when large scale and landscape these are quite effective and give off a lot of light which will enhance the look of my installation.

Projection Option 03

P5.js Patterns - another good option for the final video however would be coheasive landscape.

Projection Option 04

Animated figure patterns - not the most interesting projections however could be used to add some colour into the final video.

Projection Option 05

Touchdesigner visuals - the most effective and will form the majority of my final video.
