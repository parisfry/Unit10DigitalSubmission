PROJECTION PLANNING.

Reviewing and selecting digital outcomes for the final projection piece.

01. DITHERING VIDEOS

Dithering video experiments were less effective, as the particle movement was not clearly visible.

02. ASCII VIDEOS

ASCII videos work effectively at large scale and in landscape format, producing strong light and enhancing the installation.

03. P5.JS PATTERNS

p5.js-generated patterns are a potential final video outcome, working most effectively in a unified landscape composition.

04. ANIMATED FIGURES

Animated figure patterns offer limited projection impact but may enhance the final video through added colour.

05. TOUCHDESIGNER VISUALS

TouchDesigner-generated visuals are the strongest outcome and will make up most of the final video.
